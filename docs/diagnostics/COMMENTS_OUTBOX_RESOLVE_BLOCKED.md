---
description: Resolve cannot close a thread until its queued comment and replies reach the server. Learn how to resume delivery or dismiss the blocked entries.
---

Resolve waits for the thread's queued comment and replies to reach the server. If delivery cannot continue, Resolve stops without closing the thread.

Reconnect if you are offline. Retry a failed or held comment, or dismiss it if you no longer want to send it. Resolve the thread again after delivery finishes.
