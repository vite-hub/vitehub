Ten days in the Azores, slowly

We landed in Ponta Delgada with no car booked, which on an island with four buses a day was a mistake. The first rental desk had one car left, a manual with a cracked mirror, and we took it.

Furnas is where to slow down. The cozido is cooked for six hours in the ground by the lake, and you have to order it the day before.
